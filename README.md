<h2 id="about-soldier-field">About Soldier Field</h2>

<p>Soldier Field is located in Chicago, Illinois, near the city’s lakefront and downtown area. The stadium is best known as the home of the Chicago Bears and Chicago Fire, but it also hosts major concerts, festivals, international sports and large public events.</p>

<p>The venue has been a Chicago landmark since 1924. Its location makes it a recognizable part of the city’s skyline and event culture. For visitors coming to the Ye concert, Soldier Field offers the scale of a major stadium while still being close to central Chicago, hotels, restaurants and public transport options.</p>
